About the festival

A celebration of natural wine, people, music and atmosphere

UNFILTERED is a natural wine festival in Riga that brings together winemakers, wine lovers, importers, chefs, artists and everyone curious about low-intervention wines. It is not just a tasting event, but a full experience built around craftsmanship, discovery and community.

Expect dozens of wineries from across Europe, unlimited tasting, meet the winemaker sessions, masterclasses, great food, vinyl DJs and a unique venue with historical character. UNFILTERED is made for both experienced wine enthusiasts and people who simply want to enjoy an unforgettable day in a vibrant festival atmosphere.

The place

Modern venue with historical background

A former Riga cargo railway warehouse in the New Hanza area has been reborn as a concert hall – a platform for culture, art, music and other events.

The interior of the building has been retained to fully showcase the warehouse legacy with restored 100-year-old bricks, historical wooden beams and the original wooden doors, which once served as the gateway to the cargo station.

Winemakers

Who is in?

A selection of winemakers featured in the UNFILTERED festival material.

Bianka and Daniel Schmitt

Bianka and Daniel Schmitt are young and passionate. Their philosophy is to be in total harmony with Mother Nature. This means biodynamic farming, no chemicals, no sulphites and low intervention. No rush in the cellar: time is important for the wine to reach its full potential. Their natural wines are amazing, they go from light and glou glou, to complex and serious, from fun PetNats to crazy ciders. Some comes in a litre bottle, which is awesome news!

Champagne Moussé

Located in a small valley perpendicular to the Marne river, in the village of Cuisles, our estate has been growing a very unique Meunier on a green clay terroir since 1923. Our vineyard is spread on 3 different villages for the most part, yet all our plots are located on the same slope, all south exposed. Everyday, we are making every effort to step aside and highlight this variety and this unique terroir.

2Naturkinder

From the vineyard to the cellar countless microorganisms are part of a wine’s life story. Living in the cosmos, surrounding the vines; in the soil, grapes, juice and finally the wine. We’re also part of that story but it’s not just us writing it. We allow for rambunctiousness. For the ever-changing, incredibly complex being that ends up as wine in a bottle to be more than a resource, a product, to really be alive.

Champagne Fleury

Located in a small valley perpendicular to the Marne river, in the village of Cuisles, our estate has been growing a very unique Meunier on a green clay terroir since 1923.

Martin&Anna Arndorfer

The original taste of the grape is particularly evident in our natural wines. This creates unvarnished, honest wines that result in a unique taste experience through careful care of the vineyard, careful harvest and loving processing.

Slobodne

Slobodne is a young family winery in Majer Zemianske Sady in Slovakia. They are working on the same historical winery that once belonged to their ancestors and was recovered in the early 1990s following the tragic combination of the Second World War and 40 years of communism in the region. The family has spent the past 25 years rebuilding the farm's ecosystem and finally launched Slobodné in 2010. They are one of the leading wineries of the natural wine movement in Slovakia.

Dobra Vinice

Farming vineyards around 35 years old, Dobrá Vinice are one of the pioneers of making natural wines in the Moravia region of the Czech republic. Using amphora and traditional techniques, their wines are both elegant and rustic at the same time. Popular bottles include Creme de Park National and their Riesling wines.

Jaroslav Osicka

Jaroslav Osicka is a natural wine producer from the Moravia region in Czech Republic, who is often referred to as one of the godfathers of Natural Wine in the Czech Repoublic. Having been growing wine without chemicals for over 40 years and teaching winemaking too, Jaroslav Osicka takes a low intervention approach in the cellar as well as the vineyard of 3 hectares.

PETR KORÁB

The Petr Koráb winery can be found in Boleradice in the Velkopavlovice wine-growing sub-region, where it ecologically manages around 3 ha of vineyards. He could lead by example with his approach to viticulture. Peter is driven forward by a constantly changing nature, which brings new challenges every year and from which the wine is still not the same, but dynamic and original. Its wines can be found all over Europe and it is also very popular in renowned Copenhagen restaurants!

Syfany

SYFANY is a natural wine producer from the Moravia region in Czech Republic. Run by Jakub Zborovský alongside his wife, the Syfany bottles are good value and offer a great quality. They age their wines mostly in Acacia barrels but also use stainess steel for some of their cheaper cuvees. Popular Syfany wines include The Beast, Palasak, Freedom, Andre Rose Pet Nat and Rosé de Saignée.

Sumenjak

Sumenjak natural wine made with passion in Jakobski Dol, Slovenia. Experience the taste of nature. Explore our selection!

Vino Magula

Another great natural family winery from Slovakia! Specifically from the village of Suchá nad Parnou near the border with the Czech Republic. Vlado Magula farms there purely ecologically and in harmony with nature. It is trying to make full use of the potential of the area, which lies in the warmer climate, lower precipitation and limestone subsoil. Vlado tries to project all this into his wines, which are characterized by fullness of taste and long maturing potential. Its red wines from the Blaufrankisch, Blauer Portugeiser, Hron or Dunaj varieties, as well as orange macerated wines, are definitely worth noting.

Esencia Rural

Esencia Rural are known for experimental wines, often with Volatile Acidity and crazy flavours like Sol a Sol Airen. Run by Julián Ruiz Villanueva and his wife, the duo farm 38 hectares of often extremely old, ungrafted, dry-farmed, bush-vines, whose fruit is fermented and macerated for extended periods (from 2 to 16 months) Their entry level Pampaneo wines are good for natural wine beginners, whilst their Sol a Sol Airen is more suitable for those used to crazy wines!

Weingut Hummel

The Hummel Winery in Villány, Hungary, was founded by Horst Hummel in 1998 with the aim to produce authentic terroir wines. It has 2.5 hectares of vineyards planted with Portugieser and Merlot, Hárslevelü (Lindenleave) and Furmint. Other grape varieties are vinified from purchased grapes

Weingut Georg Toifl

Georg Toifl is a natural wine producer from the Kamptal region in Austria. He farms 3 hectares of vineyards with Grüner Veltliner, Riesling and Zweigelt. His wines are known for their purity, freshness and expression of terroir. Georg Toifl's winemaking philosophy is based on minimal intervention, allowing the grapes to express themselves naturally. His wines are a great representation of the potential of natural wines from Austria.

Vinyes Singulars

The Winery Vinyes Singulars, directed by Ignasi Segui, offers a fine selection of natural wines from the Penedès region of Catalonia. Vinyes Singulars is first and foremost a great family story. Ignasi Segui's family has been working and living on their land, located in the Penedès region, not far from Barcelona (20 km to the west), since... 1405! The Winery produces wine, oil, honey, vegetables, cereals and eggs. Everything is grown organically (certified). Enough to keep you busy!

Ārpus

Ārpus Brewing Co. is an independent microbrewery, founded in 2017 by craft beer geeks from Latvia. We brew what we love - juicy hop forward ales, fruity sour beers and bold imperial stouts - and we’re proud that Ārpus Brewing Co. is voted as #1 brewery in the Baltic States on Untappd.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you may want to know before joining UNFILTERED.

What is UNFILTERED for?

UNFILTERED is a platform for craftsmanship, community, and low-intervention winemaking. It brings together growers, wine lovers, importers, chefs, artists, and hospitality professionals around natural wine and the culture behind it.

Is UNFILTERED a conference or a festival?

UNFILTERED is a festival, not a formal conference. It is designed as a full experience built around natural wine, tasting, music, food, winemaker sessions, and community.

Where is the event taking place?

The event takes place in Riga, Latvia, in a former cargo railway warehouse in the New Hanza area, now reborn as a cultural venue.

How do you select wines?

The wine selection is built around strict low-intervention winemaking, including organic and biodynamic farming, no additives, and minimal sulfur. The festival follows the same quality-focused philosophy behind LOWINE’s award-winning natural wine selection.

What is the B2B session for?

The B2B session is designed for trade visitors to find distributors in the Baltics, build professional connections, and speak directly with winemakers in a more focused setting. It runs from 13:00 to 16:00.

What is the B2C session for?

The B2C session is for the public. It gives guests the chance to discover and taste wines, meet producers, and experience the festival atmosphere together with more than 1000 expected attendees.

Will there be masterclass sessions?

Yes. Masterclasses are part of the festival program and are presented as a way to explain and explore natural wine in more depth.

Will there be any afterparties?

Yes. The festival includes an afterparty with winemakers, and it is included in the ticket price.
